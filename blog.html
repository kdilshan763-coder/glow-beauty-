5 Haircare Habits for Healthy Shine

Great hair days start long before you sit in the salon chair. These five habits keep your hair healthy, soft and radiant between visits:

  • Don't overwash. Two to three washes a week lets natural oils nourish your strands.
  • Condition the mid-lengths and ends — never the roots — and leave it in for two minutes.
  • Always use heat protection before styling tools, even on low heat.
  • Trim every 8–10 weeks to prevent split ends from travelling up the hair shaft.
  • Book a professional treatment quarterly — keratin and spa treatments restore what daily life takes away.

Your Pre-Bridal Skincare Timeline

A wedding-day glow is built over months, not minutes. Here's the timeline our specialists recommend:

  • 3–6 months out: Begin a regular facial routine to balance and brighten your skin.
  • 1–2 months out: Add treatments targeting your specific concerns — hydration, brightening or texture.
  • 2 weeks out: Final facial — your last chance for professional-level radiance to settle in.
  • Final week: Keep it simple — gentle cleansing, moisturising and SPF. No new products, ever.

The Story of Glow Beauty

Who We Are

Beauty, Treated as an Art

Glow Beauty was founded with a simple belief: every client deserves to feel confident and cared for. Located in Horana, Sri Lanka, our salon brings premium beauty services — hair, nails, skincare and makeup — to our community with warmth and professionalism.

We never rush. Every appointment begins with a conversation — about your style, your skin, your occasion — so the result feels unmistakably you, only more radiant.

Whether it's a routine trim, a complete transformation or your wedding day, Glow Beauty is where Horana comes to glow.
